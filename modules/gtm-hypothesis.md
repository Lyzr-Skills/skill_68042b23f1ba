# GTM Hypothesis Module

Construct testable hypotheses linking audience, channel, tactic, pain, and expected metrics.

## Template
```json
{
  "hypothesis_id": "HYP-001",
  "statement": "If we target VP Marketing at 50-500 employee SaaS companies through founder-led outbound cold email focusing on pain-led reporting automation, then we can generate 10 meetings in 30 days at under $100 CAC.",
  "confidence": 0.8
}
```
Ensure statement elements can be mapped directly to experiment configurations.
